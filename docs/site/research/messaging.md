## 1. Short version

1. Players describe tracking in bookkeeping words: session, buy-in, rebuy, cash-out, hourly, true winrate. The praise word is "honest".
2. At the table the rival is the phone's Notes app, not another tracker: "It's faster to type them into the Notea app."
3. The loudest complaints in every review set are paywalls, export lock-in and lost data. Not missing features.
4. Solvers read as costly and for advanced players. Trainer-app users want the why: "explanations why my decision was wrong".
5. Competitor headlines promise outcomes ("Start Crushing Poker Today", "profitable decisions"). In the pages I read, no tracker claims to grade the hands you played. GTO Wizard's analyzer works from uploaded online hand histories.
6. Tilt is almost absent from tracker reviews. Treat it as a supporting feature, not a headline.
7. Recommended angle: HandSolved grades the hands you actually played (section 7).
8. Price: the tracker layer of the market is near-free. GTO Wizard Starter is the fair anchor for the GTO review plus trainer layer (section 5). No price is recommended here.
9. Gaps: Reddit and TwoPlusTwo could not be read. Unknowns that block copy are in section 10.

### 5.4 The fair comparison

No single competitor sells all three jobs. The like-for-like is a stack. Convert everything to per-year before comparing.

- Tracker layer. Compare against the paid "unlimited sessions" tier of the session trackers: Poker Bankroll Tracker Pro ($29.99 a year), Poker Analytics Infinity ($29.99, period not shown), Pokerbase Pro ($12.99 a month or $99.99 a year) and Poker Income ($9.99 once). Regroup gives unlimited sessions free, so the floor is $0. Leave out Pokerbase Elite and Left Pocket (features or periods unknown).
- GTO hand review layer. The fair floor is GTO Wizard Starter: Hand History Analyzer, Single Size postflop solutions, and GTO Wizard's own label "Casual players". The fair ceiling is Premium (unlimited 2-way spots, GTO Reports). Elite and Ultra are not fair: they sell custom solving, nodelocking, ICM and multiway tree building to pros, and HandSolved claims none of that (coach doc: no ICM, no rake; the README lists no custom solving or nodelocking).
- Trainer layer. GTO Wizard's Practice mode sits inside its tiers (Free is capped at 10 hands a day), so it has no separate price. Standalone trainers (Poker Academy, Heater iOS app) are partial comparisons: preflop-first, no session tracker.

Two things to know before choosing a baseline:

1. The baseline depends heavily on the layer. Paid tracker tiers run from $9.99 once to $99.99 a year on yearly or one-time terms (Pokerbase Pro on monthly billing works out to about $156 a year, derived). GTO Wizard Starter is $468 a year on annual billing or $588 on monthly billing (derived). The GTO layer is what sets the price.
2. HandSolved's README fixes the product scope (Hold'em, preflop 8-max, postflop heads-up and 3-way). That scope is closer to Starter and Premium than to Elite and Ultra.

### 5.5 Decisions needed before any price is computed

- Basis: monthly billing, annual billing per month, or per year.
- Currency, and tax included or excluded (Poker Academy is EUR net of VAT; GTO Wizard names no currency).
- Which set is "competitors": the stack, the GTO layer alone, or the trackers alone.
- Whether Free tiers (Regroup, GTO Wizard) are an anchor.
- Prices move. GTO Wizard raised Premium and Elite for new subscribers in March 2026, and its Ultra Early Bird was due to end on Oct 15.

### 5.6 Price display on a portfolio site

- The price is illustrative. COPY.md will carry it as `[DEMO PRICE]` and I will suggest a visible "concept pricing" label so no one reads it as a real offer.
- Do not print "30% cheaper than X", competitor names or competitor prices on the page. The HandSolved figure is illustrative, competitor prices change, and Premium and Elite are third-party only.
- Do not imply a free trial or free tier unless the concept includes one.

### 6.2 Limits from the coach doc

- Pots with four or more players after the flop are not solved. Those decisions are marked "not graded".
- No rake and no ICM: "chips are chips".
- Values are estimates inside a simplified game (a few bet sizes plus the hand's real sizes, sampled runouts).
- Checked against the open-source TexasSolver on three heads-up flops only (K72, T96, A83), in one line (button opens, big blind calls): about 0.28% of the pot given up per decision. Turn, river, 3-bet pots and 3-way pots are not checked yet. Use this as a trust line only if the owner approves exact wording.
- Preflop charts differ from published charts in places (UTG opens about 12% against about 15% in no-rake solves; the big blind defends tighter against a button open).
- Everyone is assumed to play GTO. Opponent-specific exploitation is switched off for now.

### 6.3 Do not claim (not in the README)

Price, free, free trial. Accounts, sign-in, sync. Offline or no-signal use. Privacy or security details (encryption, where data lives). App Store availability. Reviews, ratings, user counts. "AI". ICM. Omaha or PLO. Four-way or bigger pots graded. Rake. "Matches published charts" or any solver by name. Faster than notes. Sharing hands. Live HUD or opponent notes. Tax reports. Staking.

The README shows a server database plus a browser copy. Do not say "your data stays on your device".

## 7. Three positioning angles

### Angle 1: HandSolved keeps your numbers honest

- Idea (internal): HandSolved is where session results live, in big blinds, and stay yours to export.
- Proof: Log session; Game history by month; Insights by venue, stakes, game, day; bankroll chart; BB / $ switch; CSV and backup export; automatic backups; Regroup CSV import.
- Player language: "true winrate", "keeps you honest", export locked behind a subscription, lost data.
- Competitors: the most crowded space. Regroup gives unlimited sessions free.
- Risks: commodity. Free, offline and device-only cannot be claimed.
- Role: the trust section and the switch path.

### Angle 2: HandSolved grades the hands you actually played (recommended)

- Idea (internal): HandSolved takes the hand you played, rebuilds it on a table, grades each decision against a built-in GTO engine, then lets you replay it from the flop, turn or river and practice the spot.
- Proof: Add hand; Coach (frequencies, value of each option, pot odds, equity, range grids); Replay a hand; Practice; Hand review with the coach score saved on the hand.
- Player language: "explanations why my decision was wrong", "save the hands for future reviews", "put a memo into my iphone", solvers "for beginners" and "more affordable".
- Competitors: trackers log and chart. GTO Wizard's analyzer takes uploaded online hand histories and sits in a ladder of $39 to $289 a month on annual billing (Starter to Ultra). Trainer apps drill generic spots and reviewers ask for reasons.
- Name fit: "HandSolved" matches the idea, but it invites over-reading. Keep "solved" tied to named things: preflop charts solved for 8-max, each postflop street solved from the ranges, heads-up or 3-way.
- Risks: GTO doubt at low stakes; the accuracy limits in 6.2; trainer-accuracy complaints; no outcome promises allowed.

### Angle 3: HandSolved shows why a session went the way it did

- Idea (internal): HandSolved puts result, play quality and tilt side by side for each session and hand.
- Proof: Session grade A to F; rating pill, tilt and coach score on each hand; Insights leaks and strengths; Highlights including Tilt Tower; Goals including hands reviewed.
- Player language: "the reality of my sessions and not the emotional up and down swings", "patterns in my results based on specific events".
- Competitors: Left Pocket reviewers mention sleep metrics and mindfulness ([f-lp]). A search excerpt [S] says TiltFree tracks mood and tilt for live play ([as-tiltfree]). None seen combine decision grade, result and tilt.
- Risks: "know why" implies cause. Tilt words are thin in reviews. The README does not give the grade formula.
- Role: the emotional reason to care, and supporting proof.

### Recommendation: Angle 2

1. It is the only angle where README features meet a gap in what the pages I read promise: no tracker grades your own hands, and GTO Wizard's analyzer needs online hand-history files.
2. It matches three pains in players' own words: wanting reasons, saving hands for review, and solver cost and complexity.
3. Every claim can be backed by a README feature. No win-rate promise is needed.
4. It leaves room for Angle 3 as the reason to care and Angle 1 as the trust section.
5. Honest limits ("graded heads-up and 3-way; bigger pots marked not graded") become the trust device.

Internal positioning statement (not site copy): For poker players who track their own sessions and want to know how well they played the hands, HandSolved is the installable web app that records a hand on a poker table, grades each decision against a built-in GTO engine, and lets the player replay and practice it.

What HandSolved is not: a full solver library, a live HUD, a real-money game, or a tax or staking tool.

## 9. Words to use and words to avoid

### Use

- The app's own names: Log session, Add hand, Coach, Practice, Replay a hand, Hand review, Insights, Goals, Highlights, Bankroll chart, Equity calculator.
- Player words: session, buy-in, rebuy, cash-out, stakes, venue, bb per hour, bankroll, leaks, patterns, hand review, decision, graded, replay, study, baseline, tilt, heads-up, 3-way, stack depth, flop, turn, river, 8-max.
- Define GTO once in plain words the first time it appears.
- Specific verbs: record, rebuild, grade, replay, practice, export, restore.
- Plain, calm, specific. Reading level grade 6 to 8.
- "Profit" only as a name for a tracked metric (a Goals metric in the README).

### Avoid

- Winning promises: win more, crush, beat the games, make money, guaranteed, easy money, boost your win rate, profitable decisions, make a living. Competitors use these. Do not copy them.
- Gambling language: bet on yourself, jackpot, lucky, high roller, bonus, free chips, wager, odds of winning, win big. Avoid poker idioms as slogans (all in, bluff, ace up your sleeve). Literal use inside the product (an all-in on the felt) is fine.
- Overclaims about the coach: perfect, exact, solver-grade, optimal play as an absolute, matches published charts, validated (unless owner approves the TexasSolver line), ICM, any hand, any game, multiway beyond 3-way.
- "Solved" as a promise of certainty ("your hand, solved", "poker is solved"). The name invites it. Tie "solved" to named things.
- "AI" (not in the README, and reviewers punish it).
- Offline, private by design, on-device, secure, encrypted, cloud sync, free, trial, App Store, users, ratings, reviews. None are confirmed.
- Clinical language about tilt: cure, fix your mental game, therapy, addiction. Say "rate your tilt" and "see it next to your results".
- The old name and any "wiz" or "wizard" puns.
- "Wall of Shame" in marketing sentences. Keep it as a feature name only if the owner agrees, and never mock losses.
- Competitor names, except Regroup in the import instructions.
- Sensitive-category note: GTO Wizard's footer says "intended for adult users (18+)", and Pokerbase and Poker Academy list 18+ in the App Store. An adults-only line is normal here. Owner decides.
